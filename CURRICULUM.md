# Curriculum

The seed contains 45 evidence-oriented tasks—74 focused hours before project expansion—across five tracks:

1. Exploratory Data Analysis: vectorization, dataset auditing, cleaning, missingness, outliers, distribution and multivariate investigation, and hypothesis formation.
2. Statistics & Experimentation: simulation, distributions, sampling, intervals, tests, power, bootstrap, A/B analysis, experimental design, and causal DAGs.
3. Classical Machine Learning: supervised baselines, regularization, ensembles, clustering, PCA, leakage-safe feature engineering, validation, interpretation, error analysis, and calibration.
4. SQL & Data Engineering: windows, CTEs, query correctness, analytical modeling, idempotent pipelines, and data contracts.
5. Research & Communication: paper critique, analysis planning, reproducibility, executive reporting, data storytelling, and portfolio publication.

Every task includes objectives, duration, difficulty, type, a concrete exercise, completion evidence, priority, and skills. Completion means the specified artifact exists; it does not change demonstrated mastery automatically.

The projects are Customer Churn Prediction, A/B Testing & Causal Analysis, and NLP Analytics & Pattern Discovery. Their 17 milestones emphasize framing, integrity checks, reproducibility, error analysis, uncertainty, communication, and limitations—not merely model training.

To revise content, add new records with incremented versions, migrate task/resource associations, and set superseded records inactive. Do not overwrite or delete tasks that have user progress.
